**A fictional home fragrance product, from product and package decisions to its first ecommerce hero.** The work ran on 2026-09-10 through 2026-09-12. This page summarizes those decisions and publishes four selected images, not the complete working archive.

## Product and package

The brief was a flameless ceramic scent tile set for design-conscious shoppers buying for a small space or as a gift. That audience was a working hypothesis, not a finding from consumer testing.

| Decision | Selected concept |
| --- | --- |
| Brand | CORNER NOTE / 隅调 |
| Product | One porous ceramic scent tile, one slotted ceramic base, and one 10 ml fragrance oil bottle |
| Scents | RAIN CEDAR / 雨杉 and PAPER AMBER / 纸暖 |
| Construction | Upright folding carton, removable scent sleeve, and separated paper-fiber insert |
| Comparison dimensions | Carton: 120 mm high, 95 mm wide, 46 mm deep; tile: 70 × 50 × 10 mm |

These dimensions kept the concept comparisons consistent. They are not supplier-confirmed specifications or a dieline.

## Research and decisions

1. **Compare the product and package options.** Same-category research looked at ceramic fragrance objects, oil-and-object sets, package presentation, and the space needed for each component. The packaging options were a compact folding carton, a rigid drawer gift box, and separate object/refill boxes. The compact carton was selected to keep the set together with less bulk than a gift box.
2. **Choose a visual direction.** Three initial directions used the agreed product and package basis. After correcting comparison geometry, direction 02, **Scent Index**, was selected: two scent color schemes, diagonal sleeve blocks, and a small black-and-white index bar.
3. **Revisit the typography.** The lettering still felt too rigid for the package. Font research was reopened, and three systems were rendered with real fonts, actual copy, and the same package proportions. The product, wording, sleeve layout, and scent colors stayed fixed.

The original category research included [GALLIA RAE](https://galliarae.com/products/pietrina-home-fragrance-diffuser-set), [elemense](https://elemensefragrance.com/products/diffuser_stone), and [FAZEEK](https://www.fazeek.com.au/products/signature-fragrance-oil-bundle-scent-stone-1) product pages, recorded on 2026-09-10. They were references for formats and presentation, not evidence of mainland Chinese consumer preference. No third-party reference images are included here.

## First ecommerce hero

The selected artwork was carried into a Rain Cedar hero for the planned Taobao/Tmall product page. Image generation supplied the scene and corrected carton surface; local composition applied the real-font artwork. This publication reuses the existing output without another image call or retouch.

The hero contains four visible objects: one closed carton, one ceramic tile, one slotted base, and one capped amber bottle. The original review checked the full image and this 240 × 240 preview:

![Rain Cedar thumbnail review at 240 pixels](assets/rain-cedar-hero-thumb-240.png)

The generating agent recorded 17 concept checks at each size, 34 passes in total. That is a historical self-review, not an independent review or platform approval. Small product copy was not required to be fully readable at 240 pixels.

## What is complete

The product and package route, the two-scent front-artwork system, and **one Rain Cedar hero** are complete for this concept stage. The thumbnail is a derivative of that hero, not a second delivered image. The Paper Amber hero and the rest of the ecommerce gallery have not been produced.

Consumer preference, conversion, trademark clearance, fragrance performance, material compatibility, leakage, transport protection, labeling, and manufacturing remain unverified. This is neither a finished commercial product nor a claim of Taobao/Tmall listing compliance.

The reusable workflow is: define the product, audience, construction, and specification; research the open choices; confirm the route; compare designs on a shared basis; refine the selected typography; then carry the approved artwork into the agreed ecommerce images. This case stops at the first hero. The full gallery has not been produced, and other categories still need their own runs.
