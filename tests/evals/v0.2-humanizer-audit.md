## Findings and edits

1. The README now starts with the product and packaging workflow. Contract names appear later as implementation records instead of defining the product on first contact.
2. Ecommerce roles are described as a menu chosen during channel planning. The seven-role Quiet Pantry gallery is labeled as fixture-specific QA coverage, not a default deliverable. Its two historical direction boards are also labeled as retrospective contract evidence, not proof of post-lock generation.
3. The English introduction no longer relies on the abstract phrase "reusable value." The Chinese introduction removes the staged emphasis in "真正复用" and states the decision order directly.
4. Chinese phrases such as "真正可看" and "真正不同" were replaced with concrete comparison language.
5. Watched filler words in normative references were simplified where meaning stayed unchanged. Repeated contract requirements remain parallel when that consistency helps validation.
6. Functional tables, numbered workflow steps, schema identifiers, state names, hashes, and exact authorization language were retained. Their structure helps execution and comparison.
7. A full prose scan found no promotional conclusion, vague expert attribution, chatbot residue, decorative emoji, or em or en dash in the reviewed public scope.

## Semantic guardrails

Contract identifiers were preserved. Link targets, code blocks, hashes, dates, states, permissions, budgets, source bindings, and approval records were left unchanged unless the underlying dated evidence changed.

No release, production, legal, or platform claim was widened. Concept-stage `passed` still excludes print, regulatory, manufacturing, consumer, conversion, and marketplace approval. Runtime claims remain limited to the dated client, model, operating system, architecture, and tested installation layouts. GitHub publication still requires separate current-user confirmation.
